# About This Project

This repository is an outline of a setting for a high fantasy RPG.
The fundamental idea is to have fairly stereotypical fantasy species—humans, elves, and dwarfs—but to make them significantly different from one another in important ways, while preserving and explaining many of their sterotypical traits.
The core idea behind this is to give them very different relationships to mana, and thus magic, in ways which impact even their daily lives.
The setting would almost certainly require a custom ruleset, not just a hack of an existing set of rules.

# Mana

Mana is an intangible substance that permeates the entire world.
It grows denser further beneath the ground, and less dense at altitude.
Mana naturally shifts and moves: there are currents and breezes in the mana field.
These movements usually have minimal effect on the physical world.
However, when mana is concentrated and formed into certain shapes, it can cause spontaneous physical effects.
This is the basis of magic: a spellcaster or enchanted item manipulates the mana field to form these shapes and cause these effects.

Unfortunately, mana is also toxic to most forms of life.
At its usual densities, this a very slow effect.
A human who lives an ordinary life, avoiding the high densities of mana resulting from dangerous spellcasting or venturing deep underground, may take seventy or more years to die from the build-up of low-grade mana toxicity.
However, direct exposure to high densities of mana, such as those formed in the casting of a spell, can also cause acute toxicity.
